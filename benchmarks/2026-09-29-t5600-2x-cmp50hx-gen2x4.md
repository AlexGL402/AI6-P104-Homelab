# Dell T5600 — 2× CMP 50HX working baseline

Date: 2026-09-29

## Configuration

- Host: Dell Precision T5600
- GPUs: 2× NVIDIA CMP 50HX 10 GB
- NVIDIA driver: 610.43.03 patched CMP50 unlock
- PCIe Gen2 unlock: cmp50hx-gen2.service
- Persistence mode: Enabled
- Power limit: 145 W per GPU

Important observation:

Both cards initially did not enumerate reliably in the previous physical
slot arrangement. After swapping the two CMP 50HX cards between slots,
both cards initialized successfully together.

## PCIe layout

- GPU 0: 0000:02:00.0 — PCIe Gen2 x4
- GPU 1: 0000:04:00.0 — PCIe Gen2 x4

Both cards advertise PCIe x16 capability but currently negotiate x4.

## Direct PCIe bandwidth

CUDA 13.3 direct pinned-memory copy benchmark:

| GPU | PCIe link | H2D | D2H |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| CMP50 #0 | Gen2 x4 | 1.61 GB/s | 1.67 GB/s |
| CMP50 #1 | Gen2 x4 | 1.59 GB/s | 1.67 GB/s |

Transfer sizes 256 MiB, 512 MiB, and 1024 MiB produced effectively identical results.

Both cards report:

- LnkCap: Gen2 x16
- LnkSta: Gen2 x4

The near-identical bandwidth confirms that both CMP50 cards are operating consistently on the T5600 after swapping their physical slot positions.

## llama.cpp 1x vs 2x CMP50 — Mistral Nemo 12.25B Q4_K_M

Build:
- llama.cpp 0.5.0-dev
- build 11173
- commit 84e76d8a2

Model:
- Mistral-Nemo-Instruct-2407-Q4_K_M.gguf
- 6.96 GiB
- 12.25B params

GPU settings:
- 2x CMP 50HX 10 GB
- PCIe Gen2 x4 per GPU
- PL 145 W per GPU
- layer split 1:1 for dual-GPU tests

### pp512 / tg128

| GPUs | pp512 | tg128 |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1x CMP50 | 1337.41 tok/s | 50.31 tok/s |
| 2x CMP50 | 1310.90 tok/s | 59.60 tok/s |

Observed change:
- prompt: about -2.0%
- generation: about +18.5%

### pp4096 / tg128

| GPUs | pp4096 | tg128 |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1x CMP50 | 1270.57 tok/s | 49.79 tok/s |
| 2x CMP50 | 2060.86 tok/s | 59.49 tok/s |

Observed change:
- prompt: about +62.2%
- generation: about +19.5%

### Interpretation

For a model that already fits entirely on one CMP50, dual-GPU scaling depends strongly on workload shape.

At short prompt length (pp512), splitting the model across two GPUs does not improve prompt throughput and slightly reduces it.

At pp4096, dual-GPU prompt processing scales much better, reaching about 1.62x the single-GPU throughput.

Decode/generation improves by roughly 18-20% in both tests.

This indicates that the current dual Gen2 x4 PCIe configuration is fast enough to provide useful multi-GPU scaling for longer-context prompt processing, even though it does not provide near-2x decode scaling.
